# Code of Conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
We are committed to a harassment-free environment that respects differences and centers collaboration on facts and constructive feedback.

Unacceptable behavior includes personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, disclosure of another person's private information, and disruptive argument. Report conduct issues to the maintainers through the private channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Depending on the circumstances, maintainers may request corrections, remove content, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant.
